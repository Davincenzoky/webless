💻 Project 10: Bus / Transport Route Optimization System
1️⃣ Purpose / Problem Statement
Public or private transport systems often face inefficient routes, high fuel costs, and long travel times. This system optimizes bus or transport routes based on passenger demand and travel time, reducing operational costs and improving efficiency.
2️⃣ Input / Data Required
List of buses or vehicles (ID, capacity, route type)
List of stops/stations (location coordinates, passenger demand per stop)
Road network (distance/time between stops)
Historical passenger data (peak hours, average passengers per route)
Constraints: maximum travel time, bus capacity, operational hours
3️⃣ Modules / Features
Bus & Route Module: Register buses and assign initial routes
Stop & Passenger Data Module: Collect passenger counts per stop/time
Route Optimization Engine:
Suggest the most efficient route for each bus
Minimize total distance and travel time
Ensure buses are not overloaded
Analytics & Dashboard:
Passenger load per route
Travel time per route
Peak hour congestion points
Reports:
Optimal routes for daily operation
Passenger coverage efficiency
4️⃣ Quantitative Methods Used
Graph Theory: Model routes as nodes (stops) and edges (roads)
Transportation Model / Optimization: Minimize travel cost/time while covering all stops
Probability: Predict passenger demand at different times
Statistics: Analyze passenger distribution and peak loads
5️⃣ Output / Result
Optimized bus/vehicle routes
Dashboard showing travel time, distance, and passenger load
Reports showing route efficiency and peak hour congestion points
Cost and fuel-saving estimation
6️⃣ Suggested Technology Stack
Backend: Python (NetworkX, OR-Tools for optimization) or Java
Database: MySQL / PostgreSQL
Frontend: HTML/CSS/React
Visualization: Matplotlib, Plotly, or Map visualization (Leaflet.js / Google Maps API)
7️⃣ Optional Enhancements
Real-time GPS tracking and dynamic rerouting
Mobile app for passengers to check next bus arrival and optimized routes
Include multiple objectives: minimize fuel cost, travel time, and waiting time
Predictive analytics for future demand based on historical data

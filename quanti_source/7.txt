💻 Project 7: Hospital Patient Queue & Appointment Management System
1️⃣ Purpose / Problem Statement
Hospitals often face long patient waiting times and inefficient doctor schedules. This system manages appointments, predicts waiting times, and optimizes patient flow to improve efficiency and patient satisfaction.
2️⃣ Input / Data Required
List of doctors (name, specialization, available hours, max patients per day)
List of patients (ID, name, age, health condition)
Appointment requests (preferred date/time, doctor, urgency level)
Historical patient flow and average consultation times
3️⃣ Modules / Features
Doctor Registration Module: Add/edit doctor schedules and availability
Patient Registration Module: Register new patients and appointments
Appointment Scheduling Module:
Automatically allocate appointment slots based on doctor availability and priority
Queue Prediction Module:
Estimate patient waiting time
Predict peak hours
Analytics Dashboard:
Number of patients per doctor
Average consultation time
Peak load times
Notification Module:
Remind patients about upcoming appointments
4️⃣ Quantitative Methods Used
Queuing Theory: Estimate waiting times and queue lengths
Probability: Predict arrival rates and peak patient hours
Statistics: Calculate average consultation time, number of patients per doctor
Optimization: Allocate appointment slots to minimize waiting time and balance workload
5️⃣ Output / Result
Optimized doctor appointment schedule
Patient waiting time predictions
Dashboard of daily patient flow per doctor
Reports highlighting busy hours and patient load
6️⃣ Suggested Technology Stack
Backend: Python (Pandas, NumPy) or Java
Database: MySQL / PostgreSQL
Frontend: HTML/CSS/React
Graphs & Charts: Matplotlib, Plotly, or Chart.js
7️⃣ Optional Enhancements
Online appointment booking system for patients
Mobile app for patients to track queue and estimated waiting time
Emergency/priority handling for critical patients
Historical trend analysis for resource planning (doctors, rooms, staff)

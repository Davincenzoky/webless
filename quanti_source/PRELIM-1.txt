What Are Quantitative Methods?
Quantitative methods = using numbers, math, and statistics to analyze problems and make decisions.
It’s all about:
✔ Measuring things
✔ Turning data into numbers
✔ Finding patterns
✔ Making predictions
✔ Supporting decisions with evidence
If it can be counted, calculated, or modeled → it’s quantitative.

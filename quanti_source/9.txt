💻 Project 9: Online Exam Slot & Room Allocation System
1️⃣ Purpose / Problem Statement
Scheduling exams manually often leads to clashes, overcrowded rooms, and inefficient use of space. This system automatically assigns students to exam slots and rooms while avoiding conflicts and optimizing resource use.
2️⃣ Input / Data Required
List of students (ID, name, class/section)
List of exams/subjects (subject, date, duration, type)
List of exam rooms (room number, capacity, type: lab/classroom)
Constraints: maximum students per room, teacher availability (optional)
3️⃣ Modules / Features
Student Module: Input student details and enrollment in exams
Exam Module: Create exams with schedule, duration, and requirements
Room Module: Enter room capacities and specifications
Allocation Engine Module:
Assign students to rooms without exceeding capacity
Allocate exam slots to avoid clashes
Consider teacher invigilation constraints (optional)
Dashboard & Reports:
Room-wise exam allocation
Student-wise exam schedule
Clash detection and resolution reports
4️⃣ Quantitative Methods Used
Linear Programming / Optimization: Efficiently assign students to rooms and slots
Assignment Problem: Match students to rooms based on constraints
Constraint Modeling: Ensure no student has overlapping exams, room capacity limits
Statistics (optional): Analyze student distribution per room
5️⃣ Output / Result
Student-wise exam schedule
Room-wise allocation charts
Clash-free allocation reports
Summary of room utilization per exam
6️⃣ Suggested Technology Stack
Backend: Python (PuLP / OR-Tools for optimization) or Java
Database: MySQL / PostgreSQL
Frontend: HTML/CSS/React
Charts & Visualization: Matplotlib, Plotly, Chart.js
7️⃣ Optional Enhancements
Automatic notification to students via email/SMS for allocated exam slots
Mobile app to view personalized exam schedule
Priority scheduling for special needs or VIP students
Generate printable room-wise seating charts automatically

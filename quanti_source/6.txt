💻 Project 6: Inventory & Stock Optimization System
1️⃣ Purpose / Problem Statement
Stores, labs, and IT departments often face overstocking or stockouts because of poor inventory management. This system optimizes stock levels, predicts demand, and suggests reorder quantities to reduce costs and avoid shortages.
2️⃣ Input / Data Required
List of inventory items (item ID, name, category, unit price)
Current stock levels for each item
Historical demand/sales data
Supplier details (lead time, delivery constraints)
Minimum and maximum stock limits
3️⃣ Modules / Features
Inventory Management Module: Add/edit items and stock levels
Stock Monitoring Module: Track available quantity in real-time
Demand Forecasting Module:
Predict future demand based on historical data
Optimization Module:
Suggest reorder quantity to maintain optimal stock (Economic Order Quantity)
Minimize inventory holding cost
Reporting Dashboard:
Stock status alerts (low/high stock)
Monthly consumption trends
Supplier delivery schedules
4️⃣ Quantitative Methods Used
Statistics: Analyze historical demand trends
Probability: Estimate future demand variations
Economic Order Quantity (EOQ): Determine optimal reorder quantity
Optimization: Minimize total cost while meeting demand
Time-Series Analysis: Forecast demand patterns over time
5️⃣ Output / Result
Suggested reorder quantity for each item
Low-stock and overstock alerts
Stock usage trends over time
Cost-saving reports by minimizing excess inventory
6️⃣ Suggested Technology Stack
Backend: Python (Pandas, NumPy) or Java
Database: MySQL / PostgreSQL
Frontend: HTML/CSS/React
Charts & Graphs: Matplotlib, Plotly, or Chart.js
7️⃣ Optional Enhancements
Automatic supplier ordering based on reorder suggestions
Include perishable item tracking (expiry dates)
Predict seasonal demand trends using machine learning
Mobile app for inventory scanning and real-time updates

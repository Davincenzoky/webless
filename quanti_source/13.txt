💻 Project 13: Sales & Revenue Prediction System
1️⃣ Purpose / Problem Statement
Businesses need to forecast sales and revenue to plan inventory, staffing, and marketing. This system analyzes historical sales data and predicts future revenue, helping managers make informed decisions.
2️⃣ Input / Data Required
Historical sales data (date, product ID, quantity sold, revenue)
Product details (ID, name, category, price)
Promotions or discounts applied
Seasonal data (holidays, events affecting sales)
Optional: customer demographics or purchase patterns
3️⃣ Modules / Features
Sales Data Module: Upload or input historical sales data
Product Module: Track product details and categories
Forecasting Module:
Predict future sales and revenue using historical trends
Time-series analysis for seasonal trends
Analytics Dashboard:
Monthly, weekly, daily revenue trends
Best-selling and least-selling products
Predicted revenue for planning
Decision Support Module:
Suggest optimal inventory levels based on forecast
Identify periods of expected high or low sales
4️⃣ Quantitative Methods Used
Time-Series Analysis: Forecast sales based on past trends
Regression Analysis: Analyze relationship between promotions, price, and sales
Statistics: Mean, variance, standard deviation of sales
Probability: Predict likelihood of high or low sales periods
Descriptive Analytics: Summarize sales trends and patterns
5️⃣ Output / Result
Predicted sales and revenue for upcoming periods
Product-wise and category-wise sales trends
Visual dashboard with historical vs predicted sales
Inventory planning recommendations
6️⃣ Suggested Technology Stack
Backend: Python (Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, statsmodels) or Java
Database: MySQL / PostgreSQL
Frontend: HTML/CSS/React
Graphs & Visualization: Matplotlib, Plotly, or Chart.js
7️⃣ Optional Enhancements
Include machine learning models (Random Forest, ARIMA) for more accurate predictions
Mobile app for managers to check forecasts and make quick decisions
Integrate with inventory management for automated stock recommendations
Factor in external data like holidays, promotions, or market trends

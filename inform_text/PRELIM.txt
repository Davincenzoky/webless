What is Information Management?
Definition:
Information Management (IM) is the discipline of collecting, organizing, storing, and using information efficiently so that individuals and organizations can make informed decisions.
In practice, information management involves:
Collecting data from multiple sources
Organizing data into meaningful categories
Storing information securely for easy access
Sharing information with the right people
Maintaining accuracy and relevance of information
Ensuring compliance with laws and policies
Significance:
Supports better decision-making
Improves efficiency and productivity
Protects sensitive data
Enhances knowledge sharing in organizations

What is the Information Lifecycle?
The Information Lifecycle is the series of stages through which information passes, from creation to disposal. Managing this lifecycle ensures information remains accurate, relevant, and secure.
Stages of the Information Lifecycle:
Creation/Collection – Data is created or collected (e.g., survey results, sensor readings).
Storage – Information is stored in databases, files, or cloud systems.
Use – Accessed for decision-making or daily tasks.
Sharing/Distribution – Shared with relevant people or departments.
Archival – Stored long-term for reference or legal reasons.
Disposal/Deletion – Removed when no longer needed or obsolete.
Example:
A school collects student grades → stores them digitally → teachers analyze grades → reports shared with parents → archived after graduation → deleted after 10 years per policy.

Metadata and Its Importance
“Have you ever wondered why your photos show the date and location automatically?”
What is Metadata?
Metadata is “data about data.” It provides context, structure, and meaning to information.
Types of Metadata:
Descriptive: Author, title, date (helps find data)
Structural: File type, size, format (helps store/manage data)
Administrative: Permissions, version, retention (helps control data use)
Example:
A photo:
Descriptive → Title: “Beach Trip”
Structural → File type: JPEG, Size: 2 MB
Administrative → Created by user X, Permissions: private
Importance:
Makes searching and organizing easier
Ensures data quality and compliance
Helps maintain security
